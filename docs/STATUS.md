**A cactus hurts again 2026-09-28**: the hazard parse mapped only `Class="Spikes"`, `"Barbed"` and `"Damage"`, but stock's `BlockDamage` family also has `BlockCactus` and `BlockTrunkTip`, which override only the collision bounds (`BlockCactus.GetCollisionAABB` IL=60) or the vertex rotation and inherit the base on-contact damage (`BlockDamage.OnEntityCollidedWithBlock` IL=126). A desert POI's cactus therefore read as no hazard and hurt nothing. Both classes now map to the damage hazard. Gated by `the other BlockDamage subclasses are hazards too`.
**A turret searches on its find-target delay 2026-09-28**: `AutoTurretFireController.Init` reads `FindTargetDelay` as the cadence of the `findTarget` leg in the state machine flowchart, so a turret does not look for a target every tick. zdtd re-scanned on every pass. The turret now keeps a search countdown: the scan runs when it reaches zero and then waits the block's `FindTargetDelay`, while a target already held is revalidated (alive, transform) between searches instead of being re-searched. A block declaring none keeps the every-tick scan, which is the offline and bot path. Gated by `scenario a turret searches for targets on its find-target delay`, which pins the empty first window, the ten quiet ticks after a zombie appears and the eventual acquisition (decisive: zeroing the cooldown acquires early). Residual: the yaw/pitch turn rate (`AutoTurretYawLerp.degreesPerSecond`), which changes only how fast the client sees the muzzle come on target because zdtd snaps the server-side aim, the `E_` hit-object tag leg of `canHitEntity`, and the wake/overheat sound broadcasts.
**A turret cannot shoot behind itself 2026-09-28**: `trackTarget` (IL=121) only succeeds inside the mount's arc, `CenteredYaw +/- yawRange` and `CenteredPitch +/- pitchRange`, and `Init` parses each range as a SINGLE float and halves it (`v *= 0.5; new Vector2(-v, +v)`, IL_01EA-01FB), so a block declaring `YawRange="45"` gives a 45 degree cone. A target the turret cannot bear on accumulates `OvershootTime` and is then dropped (`AutoTurretFireController.Update` IL_03D2-0412). zdtd aimed at any target in range. The turret now keeps its deploy facing (the spawn body's yaw, so `TurretDeploy` carries it), the block stats carry both half-ranges and the overshoot time, and the fire pass gates on the wrapped bearing and pitch against the mount before it aims or fires, dropping an unreachable target and returning to Asleep. An undeclared range means no cone, which keeps the offline and bot paths firing at any bearing. Gated by `scenario a turret cannot bear on a target behind its mount`, which pins both legs: a target behind is dropped with the magazine untouched, one in front fires (decisive: ignoring the arc fires at the rear target). Residual: `FindTargetDelay`, the yaw/pitch turn rate (`AutoTurretYawLerp.degreesPerSecond`), the `E_` hit-object tag leg of `canHitEntity` and the wake/overheat sound broadcasts.
**The turret state machine is the real one 2026-09-28**: reading `AutoTurretFireController.Update` settled the shape the previous round had guessed at: Asleep accumulates `WakeUpTime` while it has a target and then goes Awake; Awake fires `BurstRoundCount` shots one `BurstFireRate` apart and then goes **Overheated** for `CooldownTime`, not a `FireRate` pause (`FireRate` is only the shot interval fallback when a block declares no burst rate); Awake with no target holds for `FallAsleepTime` before dropping back to Asleep; an unpowered or empty turret resets to Asleep. The turret carries the `TurretState` enum and the timers, and blocks.xml `CooldownTime`/`FallAsleepTime` parse through the block stats and the Extends walk. Gated by `scenario a turret wakes up, bursts, then overheats`, which pins the wake window, the three-shot burst, the cooldown hold and the next burst (decisive: dropping the Overheated transition keeps firing). Residual: the aim cone (`YawRange`/`PitchRange` against the mounted yaw), the `OvershootTime` give-up, `FindTargetDelay` and the wake/overheat sound broadcasts.
**A turret wakes up and fires in bursts 2026-09-28**: `AutoTurretFireController.Init` reads `WakeUpTime`, `BurstRoundCount`, `BurstFireRate` and `FireRate` separately, and the state machine (tile-entities-power.md section 6.2) turns through a wake-up before it fires and then fires bursts with a pause between them. zdtd conflated `BurstFireRate` with `FireRate` into one interval and never read `WakeUpTime`, so a turret fired continuously at the burst cadence from the instant it acquired a target. The block stats now carry both rates plus the wake-up time (parsed from blocks.xml `FireRate`/`WakeUpTime`, with the Extends walk), the turret keeps its burst and wake state, and the fire pass runs Asleep -> Awake on acquisition (`WakeUpTime`), then `BurstRoundCount` shots at `BurstFireRate` per burst with a `FireRate` pause before the next one; losing the target returns it to Asleep. A block that declares none of them keeps the previous single-interval behaviour. Gated by `scenario a turret wakes up then fires in bursts`, which pins the wake window and the burst-then-pause magazine (decisive: dropping the pause fires 6 shots instead of 3). Residual: the `Overheated` state (`CoolOffTime`/`OvershootTime`) and `FallAsleepTime`.
